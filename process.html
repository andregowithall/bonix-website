Our Model

From First Conversation to Delivery

A structured, eight-step path to production — built to support brands managing anything from a single launch to an ongoing multi-line pipeline.

01

Discuss Intent

Share your needs & goals
We start with a working session to understand your product vision, timeline and volume expectations.
02

Sign Contract

Terms & agreement confirmed
Scope, pricing and IP terms are formalized before any development work begins.
03

Confirm Product

Formula & sample approval
Ideal for brands managing multi-SKU production pipelines — formulas and reference samples are reviewed and signed off.
04

Confirm Packaging

Design & materials selected
Structural and artwork files are finalized and proofed for print.
05

Apply for Filing

Regulatory registration
We handle documentation for the markets you're launching into.
06

Purchase Materials

Sourcing & procurement
Raw materials and packaging components are ordered against your confirmed spec.
07

Begin Production

GMPC-standard manufacturing
Full-batch production proceeds under continuous in-line quality control.
08

Complete Delivery

Export & door delivery
Freight is arranged and tracked through to your destination.
